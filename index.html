How we work

How a job runs, start to finish.

Plenty of masons think only certain jobs need a real footing. We pour one under everything. The ground moves; our work should not.

i

Walk the property

A free, on-site consultation. We look at grade, drainage, sun, the house, and how you actually live outside.

ii

Design together

Start from your idea or from our portfolio. We sketch, choose stone and joint style, and agree on every detail before a shovel moves.

iii

Pour the foundation

Concrete footings, cured for days, not hours. The earth shifts. Our work should not.

iv

Set stone by stone

Each piece is chosen, cut and placed by hand. Joints are tight, pitched to shed water, and finished the way you chose.

v

Light the fire

We leave the property immaculate. You get a fireplace, wall or patio that will still be standing for the next family.
