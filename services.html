02 · Restauri

Historic restoration & repointing

Bucks County is full of 18th- and 19th-century fieldstone houses, and many are hiding under stucco. We reveal and restore original stonework, and we repoint walls, chimneys, fireplaces, patios and pillars that weather and moisture have worn down.

  • Stucco and plaster removal to expose original stone
  • Repointing: joints raked (concaved) and refilled with the right mortar
  • Your choice of concave joints (smooth, blended) or convex joints (raised, pronounced)
  • Stone replacement where joints can no longer be saved
  • Historic steps, walls, chimneys and flagstone patios

Good questions

Things people ask before they call.

Real stone or veneer?
We prefer natural stone: it is what our family has worked with for four generations and it lasts longest. Veneer is a lighter, thinner, man-made stone that can be a good choice on certain structures or budgets. Either way, it gets a real foundation and is set by a mason who plans every piece. We will walk you through both on site.
How does an estimate work?
Call or send the form and we come to the property. We talk through what you want, look at grade, drainage and access, and follow up with a written estimate. The consultation and estimate are free.
How long does a fireplace or patio take?
A walkway can be a few days; an outdoor fireplace roughly a week or two after the foundation cures; a full entertainment patio with walls, steps and a fireplace runs longer. We never rush the footing. Concrete cures for days before stone goes on it, because that is what keeps the work level for decades.
What is repointing, and does my stone house need it?
Repointing means raking out failing mortar joints and refilling them with the right mortar. If you see crumbling joints, gaps, loose stones or water getting in, it is time. We will also tell you honestly if some stones need replacing rather than pointing.
Do you work outside Bucks County?
Yes. We regularly work across Montgomery County, the Main Line, Chester County, Philadelphia, Princeton and central New Jersey, and we have taken projects in New York and Delaware. If your county is not listed, ask.
Will the job site be a mess?
No. Clients tell us the same thing every time: the property is kept immaculate during construction and left clean when we are done.
