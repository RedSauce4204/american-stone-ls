Case study · Restoration

From stucco to stone.

A Doylestown family hired us to replace siding with stone walling. Behind a recent addition we found a foundation of cinderblock, wood and loose stone that could not carry a laundry room. We recommended taking it down, and under the stucco of the main house we found the original fieldstone. The owners chose to expose and repoint it, and we expanded the patio in bluestone.

Location
Doylestown, PA
Scope
Demolition, stone reveal, repointing, bluestone patio
Result
A house that looks the way it did 150 years ago

Case study · Walkways

An entrance walkway, rebuilt.

The original slate walkway had cracking joints and weeds growing through, a sure sign the base had failed. We lifted every stone, replaced the decayed concrete foundation, planned a new pattern that salvaged what could be saved, and set each piece pitched to drain. A stain finished the job and protects the stone.

Location
Doylestown, PA
Material
Salvaged and new flagstone on a new concrete base

Ours, and the other guy's.

A client dropped their first mason halfway through a gazebo project because the stone pillars were coming out wrong. We were hired to build the outdoor gas fireplace properly. On the left, our veneer work. On the right, the previous contractor's, on the same property.

Veneer is thinner and lighter than full stone, but it still demands a real foundation, a planned pattern and a mason who cares where every piece lands. Some quarries advertise it as something anyone can install. These two photos are the argument against that.
